instructions between the previous call (or `add esp`) and that call in the retail image, measured
with ndisasm over the exact body extent. A push can be deferred to the next callee, so
`Retail pushes` is an upper bound on the callee's arity; a `DIVERGENCE` row is a site where the two
disagree. Divergences are reported, never silently corrected: IGI-RE is a functional rewrite and
sometimes widens a call to pass what the original meant, while retail pushes fewer arguments and
lets the callee read stale stack.
